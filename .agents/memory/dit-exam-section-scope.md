---
name: DIT exam-only sections
description: Exam grouping is independent of registered classes, with one current membership and preserved history.
---

DIT exam sections (for example Super Section, Green, and Red) are exam-only groups. They must never replace the student's registered parent class/section or appear in registration or attendance selectors.

**Why:** The user explicitly requires the parent section to remain the institute and attendance record while students can move freely between exam sections.

**How to apply:** Use current exam membership for exam roster and analytics filtering. A move replaces current membership, not the parent class, and must retain a dated section-change history. Keep all existing results; record section-at-entry for new results without rewriting that historical section on later edits or moves. Do not invent historical sections for results entered before exam grouping existed.
