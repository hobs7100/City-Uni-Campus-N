---
name: Full semester course catalog
description: User's standing scope for course selection while starting or editing semesters.
---

Every class's semester must offer the full subject/course catalog in its Add Course dropdown, including when creating or starting a semester. Do not restrict the semester curriculum to the class's department or program.

**Why:** The user explicitly requested “all subject/courses should be displayed in every class's semester (a full catalog available in dropdown).”

**How to apply:** Preserve campus-wide course selection in both semester creation and existing-semester editing, and allow cross-department course links on the server. Exclude already-linked courses when adding to an existing curriculum to prevent duplicates.
