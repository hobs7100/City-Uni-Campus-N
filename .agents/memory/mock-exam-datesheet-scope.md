---
name: Mock Exam datesheet scope
description: Keep the campus-wide preparatory Mock Exam schedule separate from DIT mock results and Mid Exam records.
---

The Mock Exam datesheet under the main Results area is a campus-wide preparatory schedule for the final term, not the DIT-only mock-result series. Keep its schedule independent of both DIT mock results and Mid/Re-Mid datesheets. Saving the schedule makes it visible to relevant teachers and students, following the Mid datesheet workflow.

**Why:** The project already uses “mock” for a separate DIT results feature. Reusing that result series would limit ordinary classes and confuse exam preparation dates with graded records.

**How to apply:** Use the class-semester-course schedule for the main Mock Exam tab, and keep teacher/student datesheet views separate from DIT mock result entry. Treat a semester's saved Mock datesheet as one unit when listing or deleting it.
